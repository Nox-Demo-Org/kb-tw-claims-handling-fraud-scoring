# Business requirement: Policyholder renewal date on home screen

## The request
"Test only: let policyholders see their renewal date on the home screen"

## Problem
When policyholders sign in to their online account, the main home screen does not show when their current policy expires or renews. To check this date, customers must search through downloadable policy schedules, dig through old emails, or call customer support. 

This creates unnecessary friction for customers who simply want to check their cover period or plan their household budget. It also drives avoidable inbound phone calls and support tickets into our customer service team, taking time away from handling complex claims and customer inquiries.

## Who is affected
- **Active policyholders**: Any customer logging in to review their insurance policy, especially those within a few months of their policy end date.
- **Customer service team**: Support staff who currently take repeat calls and messages from policyholders asking for their renewal date.

## What should change
Policyholders will now see their upcoming renewal date clearly displayed on the main home screen right next to their active policy details. They should see the exact date their cover renews without needing to download documents, navigate deeper into account settings, or contact support.

## What "done" looks like
A policyholder logs into the member portal or app, arrives on the home screen, and can immediately read their renewal date alongside their policy name and policy number. Inbound customer service queries asking "When does my policy renew?" begin to drop.

## Examples
- **Sarah Jenkins (Home Insurance)**: Sarah wants to know when her annual cover ends so she can budget for the year ahead. 
  - *Before*: Sarah logs in, sees her policy number and cover level, but has to search through policy PDF documents to find the date.
  - *After*: Sarah lands on the home screen and sees "Renewal date: 14 November 2026" displayed clearly in her policy overview card.
- **Marcus Vance (Contents Insurance)**: Marcus calls customer support to ask when his cover runs out after moving home.
  - *Before*: The support agent looks up the account while Marcus waits on the phone.
  - *After*: The agent directs Marcus to his account home screen, where Marcus can see "Renews on: 03 March 2027" instantly, saving both of them time.
